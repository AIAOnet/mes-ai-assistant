# Machine 01 High-Pressure Response Procedure

- Document ID: SOP-M01-HP-001
- Version: 1.0
- Machine: MACHINE-01
- Alarm type: HIGH_PRESSURE
- Owner: Maintenance Engineering
- Status: Approved for RAG demonstration only

## Purpose

This procedure describes the safe operator and maintenance response when Machine 01 reports a high-pressure condition in the hydraulic circuit.

## Immediate operator response

1. Do not restart the machine while the HIGH_PRESSURE alarm is active.
2. Confirm that Machine 01 is stopped and the motor speed is zero RPM.
3. Keep personnel clear of hydraulic hoses, fittings, and stored-energy components.
4. Record the displayed pressure, alarm time, and active production order.
5. Notify the maintenance team.

## Isolation and inspection

Only authorized maintenance personnel may perform this section.

1. Apply the approved lockout/tagout procedure.
2. Isolate the hydraulic energy source.
3. Verify that stored hydraulic pressure has been safely relieved.
4. Inspect hoses and fittings for damage, restriction, leakage, or deformation.
5. Inspect the pressure-relief valve and confirm that it is not obstructed.
6. Check the pressure sensor connector and wiring for visible damage.

Never loosen a hydraulic connection while the circuit is pressurized.

## Conditions required before restart

Machine 01 may be restarted only when all of the following are true:

- The cause of the excessive pressure has been inspected.
- Hydraulic pressure has returned to the approved operating range.
- No leak or damaged component is present.
- All guards are installed.
- Lockout/tagout has been removed by the authorized person who applied it.
- The HIGH_PRESSURE alarm is no longer active.
- Maintenance has released the machine for operation.

## Controlled restart

1. Clear personnel from the machine area.
2. Start Machine 01 at the normal operating setting.
3. Observe hydraulic pressure for at least two minutes.
4. Stop the machine immediately if pressure rises abnormally or the alarm returns.
5. Record the restart result in the maintenance log.

## Escalation

Do not continue production if the high-pressure alarm repeats after a controlled restart. Keep the machine stopped and escalate the issue to a maintenance engineer for further diagnosis.
